## Scope

Candor desktop app, all screens (Operate mode): first-run setup, Today, Jobs, Applications,
Profile, Settings, and the assistant run panel. Audience: non-technical, often anxious job
seekers on a Windows laptop. Task: know what to do next, find strong-fit jobs, get a
checked application package, mark it sent. Constraints: the app never submits; postings
are untrusted; no invented metrics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Jobs are a ranked table with two drawn lines that mean something: at 80 the
assistant drafts, below 60 it does not. The app refuses the category default of a grey
SaaS dashboard of same-size cards with one blue accent.

OWN-WORLD: The printed league table and form guide. Deep bottle-green shell rail, cool
white work ground, kit-yellow for the primary action and the 80 line, claret for blocked
and gate-fail rows, ink near-black. Barlow Condensed for headings and every number
(tabular), Segoe/system sans for prose. Ruled rows, tabular figures, position numbers in
the left column, small result chips in a row (the form guide) for each application's
journey.

STORY: The person opens the app and reads one line saying what to do today, one big
button to do it. They see their jobs ranked by fit with the line that says which ones are
worth drafting, prepare one, watch the assistant work step by step, read the PDFs and mark
it sent. They believe it because every blocked claim is shown, not hidden.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail 232px bottle green: wordmark, five nav items with counts, the
engine status at the foot. Main: a Today heading with the date, one sentence of status,
the primary next action as a full-width yellow-ruled action row with its button on the
right; beneath, the numbered action list; right column (>=1200px) shows the jobs league
table preview with the 80 line drawn across it. Mobile: rail becomes a top bar with a
menu button; the action row stays first.

FORM: Printed league table and form guide, candidate 5 of 7, seed key a2933040.
Signature move: the threshold rules drawn across the ranked jobs table (80 ready to
draft, 60 not worth drafting) and the form-guide chips for each application's stages.

RAISES: from the Japanese dense web (declined): every panel carries a compact header tab
with its count. From the cassette j-card (declined): the page budget (CV 2 pages, letter 1)
and the honest time estimate shown as a capacity bar on each package. From the Factory
catalogue (declined): every job and application has one stable number used everywhere.
From the zoo guide map (competitive on audience identification): colour commits at region
scale, the rail and run panel are flat colour fields, not accents. From the split-flap
board (declined): a state change restyles a row in place and never moves its columns.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review,
the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
