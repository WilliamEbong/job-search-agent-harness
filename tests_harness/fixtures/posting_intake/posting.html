About the role

Fjordlys Kraftverk AS operates 14 small hydroelectric plants along the western fjords and sells the output into the Nordic day-ahead market. The Grid Operations team forecasts generation, balances positions and reports on network losses. We are hiring a senior analyst to own the forecasting pipeline end to end.

What you will do

  • Own the day-ahead generation forecast and its accuracy reporting.
  • Build and maintain data pipelines over SCADA telemetry and market data.
  • Quantify network losses and produce the monthly regulatory return.
  • Work with plant engineers to turn operational questions into measurable ones.
  • Mentor two junior analysts.

Requirements

  • Five or more years in an analytical role working with time-series data.
  • Strong SQL and Python (pandas); comfortable owning a production pipeline.
  • A degree in a quantitative discipline, or equivalent demonstrated experience.
  • Working proficiency in English. Norwegian is not required.
  • Eligibility to work in Norway.

Nice to have

  • Exposure to power markets, Nord Pool, or grid balancing.
  • Experience with dbt or a comparable transformation framework.
  • Any Scandinavian language.
