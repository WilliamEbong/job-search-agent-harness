## Environmental Data Analyst

**Rivermouth Environmental Consulting** — Winnipeg, Manitoba (hybrid, 3 days on site)
**Requisition:** 4471
**Employment type:** Permanent, full-time
**Salary band:** 68,000 – 79,000 CAD per year
**Closing date:** 2026-09-15

### About the role

Rivermouth supports municipal and industrial clients across the prairies with water
quality monitoring, regulatory reporting and environmental compliance. We are looking for
an Environmental Data Analyst to own the data side of our monitoring contracts — from
field data arriving in the office through to the reports our clients submit to
regulators.

You will work alongside field crews and laboratory partners, and you will be the person
who notices when a dataset is telling a different story from the one in the summary.

### Responsibilities

- Own QA/QC for incoming field and laboratory data across multiple monitoring contracts.
- Produce recurring regulatory and stakeholder reports to deadline.
- Maintain sampling site records and spatial data.
- Work with field crews on sampling protocol and data-capture improvements.
- Investigate anomalies and follow them to a defensible explanation.
- Present findings to non-technical client contacts.

### Requirements

- Degree in environmental science, hydrology, biology, statistics or similar.
- Three or more years working with environmental monitoring data.
- Demonstrated experience with laboratory data workflows and chain-of-custody
  documentation.
- Advanced spreadsheet skills for QA/QC and reporting.
- Experience with GIS software for spatial data and mapping.
- Strong written communication — this role writes documents that regulators read.
- Valid driver's licence; occasional travel to field sites (roughly 15%).

### Preferred

- Python or R scripting for data processing.
- Experience coordinating volunteer or community monitoring programmes.
- Familiarity with provincial water quality guidelines.
- Exposure to dashboarding tools such as Power BI.

### What we offer

- Salary band above, reviewed annually.
- Hybrid working, three days on site in our Winnipeg office.
- Professional development budget, including certification support.
- Four weeks' vacation.

### How to apply

Submit a resume and cover letter through our careers portal, quoting requisition 4471.
We review applications as they arrive and expect to interview in September.
